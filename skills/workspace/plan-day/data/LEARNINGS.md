## Preferences
- _(confirmed, 3 days — FIX HOLDS)_ Short content posts slip when standalone, but ship when
  batched. 06-15 both 15-min posts pushed; 06-16 the same two posts batched into one 20-min
  slot adjacent to the work session → **both shipped**; 06-17 a batched 20-min launch-posts
  slot **shipped again** (the one project win in the focus window that day). **How to apply:**
  keep batching content posts into a single protected slot adjacent to the work session —
  never schedule them as separate standalone 15-min items. This is now the reliable pattern.
  **Caveat (06-19):** a batched post tied to a *not-yet-shipped* build still slips — the Tessera
  LinkedIn post had nothing to announce when the Tessera build didn't ship, so it rolled with it.
  Batching beats standalone, but only point a post at an artifact that already exists; if the
  build is uncertain, decouple the post or make it a "build-in-public progress" angle instead.

## Energy
- _(tentative, 2 days)_ Afternoon focus window (≈13:00–18:00) is where real work lands **when the
  work is concretely scoped**. 06-15 peak 15:45–17:45 (dashboard build); 06-16 peak 13:00–14:30
  (Tessera v2). Front of the window works as well as the back — slot the ★top item early. Caveat
  from 06-17: the window itself isn't magic — that day both afternoon project blocks were vaguely
  scoped and got shelved, and only the batched content slot delivered. The window rewards a clear
  target, not just the time of day.

## Patterns
- _(tentative, 1 day)_ Late-night overflow (22:30–00:00) is unreliable — both research
  blocks parked there on 06-15 went undone. 06-16 left it empty by design and nothing was
  lost. **How to apply:** treat 22:30–00:00 as truly optional; keep important work in the
  afternoon window.
- _(confirmed, 3 days)_ Open-ended research fails to land; reframing it as a concrete decision
  rescues it. 06-16 the launch/marketing-channel research came back unsatisfactory; 06-17 the redo
  wasn't done and the whole thread was **put on hold indefinitely**. 06-19 the YT/IG thread —
  reframed from "research" into a pinned **go/no-go decision + 3 ideas** — **landed** in its block.
  **How to apply:** don't schedule vague "research X" blocks. Reframe as a single concrete question
  with a named deliverable ("go/no-go + N ideas", "pick one of A/B/C"); when it stays fuzzy it gets
  deferred then abandoned, when it's a decision it ships.
- _(confirmed, 3 days)_ Vaguely-scoped focus items are fragile; pinning a finishable target
  rescues them. 06-17 "Visage AI Project — work toward completion" (no concrete sub-goal) was
  paused indefinitely without a single session. 06-18 Tessera was re-blocked **with a pinned
  finishable target** and **landed in 75 min**. 06-19 the two blocks with concrete done-lines
  (Remotion "one rendered video end-to-end", YT/IG "go/no-go + 3 ideas") both shipped, while
  Tessera — whose done-line *deferred* the pin ("pin which one when you start") — was the only
  build that slipped. **How to apply:** every focus block needs a specific, finishable target
  pinned *at plan time*, not "work toward completion" and not "decide when you start" — the
  deferred pin behaves like no pin.

- _(confirmed, 2 days)_ Not every slip is a planning failure — check the cause before learning from
  it, and roll externally-blocked blocks forward as-is. 06-18 the Remotion-skill and YT/IG-decision
  blocks were both missed to important personal matters eating the afternoon, not fragile scoping.
  Both were rolled forward unchanged to 06-19 and **both shipped** there. **How to apply:** when a
  block slips, ask why; if the cause is external (life/interruptions), roll it forward as-is and
  don't penalise the task's structure — a sound, concrete block that missed for external reasons
  lands on the next reach. The 06-18→06-19 carry-overs are the proof.
