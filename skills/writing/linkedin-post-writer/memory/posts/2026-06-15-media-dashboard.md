The question I get most lately: "How do you keep up with AI?"

Honest answer: I stopped trying.

The problem was never finding information. It's the firehose — 20 tabs, 5 apps, a feed that refills faster than anyone can read it.

So a few months back I built myself a filter, not another feed.

One page that pulls everything I actually follow into a single place:

→ 19 YouTube channels (AI builders + podcasts) — new uploads land on their own, nothing to scroll for
→ A daily news digest across AI/ML, politics and geopolitics — an LLM reads the last 24 hours and hands me the summary, not 60 headlines
→ AI tools & launches — Show HN, GitHub trending and Product Hunt, trimmed down to the few that matter
→ An instant ping the moment something new drops

All free RSS under the hood. No paid APIs. Built for an audience of one: me.

The shift that mattered wasn't the tech. It's that I stopped reacting to a feed. I open one page, the noise is already cut, and I spend the time thinking instead of doomscrolling.

Turns out "staying updated" isn't about consuming more. It's consuming less, on purpose.

So let me flip the question:

How do YOU stay updated on AI — what's actually in your stack?
